Cross-platform fitness game (Expo/React Native, TypeScript, Supabase, Strava) where real workouts
are the only way to grow a collection of 28 creature species — no mechanic ever sells creature
power. This chapter pivoted the visual direction from pixel art to soft-3D: an image-to-3D asset
pipeline (gpt-image-2 → Meshy) and a repeatable per-species styling seam. 43 commits in the repo
from 2026-06-09; `projects/pips.md` records an earlier design start of 2025-11-29 (?) that no
scanned artifact corroborates.
