Next.js/TypeScript reporting platform over a SQL reporting warehouse: provider utilization,
billing performance, accounts receivable and provider productivity. Includes HR/payroll data
reconciliation, role-scoped access control, and a golden-file regression workflow so report
numbers can't silently drift. 138 commits and still active.
